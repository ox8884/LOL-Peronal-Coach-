About ARAM: Mayhem Augments

Augments are special power-ups unique to ARAM: Mayhem. You select augments at levels 3, 7, 11, and 15.

Each selection offers three random augments with one reroll available per selection. Augments come in three rarities:

  • Prismatic - Most powerful, game-changing effects
  • Gold - Strong effects that enhance your playstyle
  • Silver - Basic stat boosts and utility

New in Patch 26.3: Many augments now belong to Augment Sets. Collecting multiple augments from the same set unlocks powerful bonus effects!
